# Third-party notices

RegBridge depends on third-party Python, JavaScript, container, and operating-system packages. Their names and pinned or resolved versions are recorded in `backend/pyproject.toml`, `backend/uv.lock`, `frontend/package.json`, `frontend/package-lock.json`, and the Dockerfiles.

Those components are not relicensed by this repository. Each remains subject to its own copyright and licence terms. Before redistribution or deployment, generate a dependency licence inventory from the locked dependency graphs and review the corresponding upstream notices.
